Principles

Design language for the car-controls sheet on the Ocean's portrait 1080 × 1920 screen. Tesla's in-car interface is the reference; the 3D Ocean stays on screen in every view.

  1. What Tesla's screens actually do. A flat ground, near-black at night and warm light grey by day. Controls are cells drawn with a hairline border, not a fill. Radii are small. Type is regular weight; the hierarchy is in size and grey. Icons are thin line drawings. Color is almost absent: a chosen thing turns its glyph blue, a segmented control lifts a pale pill, and a screen has at most one blue-filled button.
  2. The car is the interface. The sheet holds only what the car can't show by itself. Doors swing, windows sink, seats glow and the port opens on the model; the sheet never repeats that in a read-out block.
  3. Choose, don't toggle. A choice between named states is a segmented control (Off · Parking · On · Auto). A switch is for a preference you set once.
  4. Cells for what you do while driving. A tile is a 120 px cell you can hit without looking: A/C, defrost, heated wheel, fold mirrors. Rows are for settings you reach when parked.
  5. The number first. A category opens on the one number that matters, light and large but not huge: the set temperatures, the charge level, the lock. Then the controls, then the preferences.
  6. Group by what it does to the car. Climate holds the seat heaters, as Tesla's does. Doors & Windows holds the lock, the windows, the liftgate and the mirrors.
  7. One language for live, sent and off. An unavailable control is greyed, not hidden, and says why on tap. The dock says whether changes reach the car.
  8. Scale by frame, not by pixel. Everything is rem against a 1080-wide frame. No backdrop blur over the 3D view.

Zones

  • Status strip, 96. Gear, speed and telltales left; time, outside temperature and the link right. Replaces the floating status card while the controls are open.
  • Stage. From the strip to the sheet. Chips on parts, badges in the overview. The camera refits on every detent change.
  • Sheet. Ground color, a 12 px radius and a hairline at the top, no shadow. Peek (448) shows the handle, the title and the hero. Half (848) is the default. Full (1360) is for long lists and leaves 352 px of car. Drag the handle or tap it to step.
  • Dock, 112. Flat, full width, a hairline on top. Ten car-part categories as icon + 14 px label, a divider, More for the five system categories. The state tag at the left edge; ✕ at the right.
  • Gutter 40 at the screen edges; 8 between cells; 32 between groups.

Color

Two surfaces and a hairline. Text in Tesla's grey, not black. One blue; it appears as a glyph, a thin fill line or a single primary button, never as a wash.

Ground, cells and ink

Accent and semantics

The accent marks a chosen glyph, a slider's fill, a window that is open, and the one primary button. Heat is seat and wheel heat; cool is A/C and defog; ok is charging, locked and live.

Type

The system face (Roboto on the car) at 300, 400 and 500 only, with a little tracking on labels. Tabular numerals wherever a value changes.

Measure

An 8 px grid. Hit targets are 72 at least. One radius, 8, for every cell; 12 for the sheet's top.

Icons

Line icons on a 24 grid at a 1.6 stroke, round caps (the HUD's ICONS, drawn thinner here). 32 in tiles and the dock, 28 in a segment, 24 in a row. Resting glyphs are grey; a chosen glyph is blue; nothing is two-tone.

Components

Each one is a class in design.css. Tap them: the guide wires the states the way carcontrols.js would.

Segmented control .fo-seg

One choice from two to five. A grey track; the chosen segment is a lifted white pill (lighter grey at night). .stack puts an icon over a short label. A segment can carry a color dot.

Tiles .fo-tiles .fo-tile

Equal cells, four to a row, icon over label. On: the glyph goes blue (heat red, cool blue) and the border darkens a touch; the cell stays white. .primary is the one blue-filled button a screen may have. .off is greyed.

Rows .fo-rows .fo-row and the switch .fo-switch

Preferences. Hairline dividers, nothing filled. Label, an optional second line, the control at the end: a switch, a value with a chevron, or a segmented control (under the label when it has more than three options).

Heat glyph .fo-heat

A seat with three waves above the cushion, as Tesla's heated-seat symbol; each level fills one wave red. Tap cycles Off → 1 → 2 → 3 → Off. The same glyph is the rear seats' chip and the steering wheel's.

Hold buttons .fo-hold

Held, a button keeps the car moving and fills blue; let go, it stops. The car's reported position at the end.

Windows .fo-wins .fo-win

The eight openings as cells in two rows of four, front row first, left to right as in the car. An open one shows its percentage in blue with a thin line along the bottom for how far. Tap: all the way the other way; hold: jog.

Equalizer .fo-eq

Hairline rails for the five bands, zero marked, a small round thumb, the value above.

Seat adjuster .fo-seatpos

The seat from the side as a line drawing in its position, and the four jog pairs laid out like the switch on the seat's flank.

Chips on the car .fo-chip

Tesla labels the car with grey text on a thin leader line. Over the moving 3D view the text needs a ground, so a chip is a dark translucent cell with a hairline leader. A pair chip has the door and its window; a chosen glyph is blue.

Screens

Four categories at frame size, the stage above the sheet, the dock below. Change the detent to see the stage give way. The outline stands for the 3D car.

Climate Set temperatures first. Comfort cells. Fan. Airflow as icon segments. The four seat heaters, as Tesla keeps them on the climate screen. Departure last.
Doors & Windows The lock as a line, not a hero box. The window cells replace seven Up/Down rows; the chips on the car do the same per door. Liftgate. Body cells. Locking preferences as rows.
Energy Charge, range and status, the limit marked on the bar. The limit slider under it, current as a stepper, Stop charging as the one primary. Schedule and V2L below.
Driving Drive mode, regen, accelerator and steering as segments, as Tesla's Pedals & Steering is. The assists as cells.
Lighting, Display, Audio Sheet bodies only.
Seats In landscape, a card beside each front seat. In portrait there is no room for two cards beside the car, so the same groups go in the sheet and only the heat chips stay on the seats.
